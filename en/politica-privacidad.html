What data we collect and why

Through this website we only process the data you provide voluntarily:

Contact formName, email address, telephone number and the content of your messageTo answer your enquiry and get back to you

Please do not send clinical information through the form or by email. Health data carries reinforced protection and email is not a secure channel. If you need to discuss a health problem, simply tell us you would like an appointment and we will deal with it in consultation or by telephone.

Legal basis for processing

We process your data on the following legal bases, set out in article 6 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR):

  • Your consent, given when you tick the box on the form and when you accept cookies. You may withdraw it at any time.
  • Pre-contractual measures taken at your request, where your enquiry is an appointment request.
  • The legitimate interest of the controller in responding to communications addressed to it.

How long we keep your data

We keep form data for as long as needed to deal with your enquiry and, after that, for the limitation period of any liability arising from that communication. Analytics data is kept in aggregated form according to the retention periods configured in the analytics tool.

If you go on to become a patient of the clinic, your clinical records are governed by Spanish healthcare legislation and not by this policy, which covers the website only.

Your rights and how to exercise them

You may at any time exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction of processing and portability, and withdraw any consent you have given us.
