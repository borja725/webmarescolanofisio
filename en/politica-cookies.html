Cookie policy

What a cookie is

A cookie is a small text file that a website stores on your device when you visit it. It is used to remember information about your visit, such as your chosen language, or to measure in aggregate how the site is used.

Technical cookies are essential for the site to work and do not require your consent. All others — analytics, personalisation or advertising — may only be set if you accept them.

The first time you enter the site, a notice lets you accept or reject non-essential cookies. Rejecting is as easy as accepting, and if you reject them the site continues to work normally.
